Our story

From marketing agency to video-first studio

Who we are, how we got here, and why video is at the centre of everything we do.

  1. 01

    Where we started

    AdSource began as a digital marketing agency, running ads and social media for local businesses and online brands.

  2. 02

    What we noticed

    Campaign after campaign, the same thing happened: the ads and posts that performed best were always built around great video.

  3. 03

    Where we are now

    So we made video the heart of what we do. Today we edit, animate and produce for YouTubers, coaches, local businesses and growing brands — with the marketing to grow it built in.
