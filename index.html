Ask a question
How long does an edit take?

Shorts and Reels are usually ready in 24–48 hours. A standard YouTube video (10–20 minutes) takes about 3–5 working days, and UGC ads take about 5–10 working days, depending on creator filming and the number of variations. Need it faster? Ask about priority delivery.

How many revisions do I get?

Every project includes two rounds of revisions. Most clients are happy after the first round. Extra rounds or big changes to the original brief can be added for a small fee — we'll always tell you before any extra cost.

Which file formats do you accept and deliver?

Send us footage in any common format — MP4, MOV, MXF, ProRes, or straight from your phone or camera. We deliver in MP4 (H.264 or H.265) by default, sized for each platform: 16:9 for YouTube, 9:16 for Reels, Shorts and TikTok, and 1:1 or 4:5 for feed posts. Project files and other formats are available on request.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on video length, how much footage there is, and the level of graphics or animation. We give you a clear, fixed quote before we start, and offer monthly packages for regular YouTube and short-form content. Tell us about your project for a free quote.

How do I send you my footage?

Upload your files to Google Drive, Dropbox or WeTransfer and share the link with us. Include any notes, logos, fonts, music or example videos you like, and we'll take it from there.
